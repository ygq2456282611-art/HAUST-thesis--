{\zihao{-4}\songti
脑卒中作为全球范围内长期残疾的主要致因之一，其幸存者中约有60\%至73\%的比例伴有各类视觉功能障碍~\cite{rowe2021ivis,liu2023vision,zheng2025stroke,sun2024pursuit}，涵盖同向性偏盲、眼球运动障碍以及单侧空间忽略等表现，对患者的日常生活自理能力和康复预后均构成了显著影响。现阶段的康复干预手段面临着一个两难处境：传统人工训练方式缺乏标准化的操作规范与量化记录机制，而VR/AR等高端技术设备则因成本居高不下且操作流程复杂，难以向居家康复场景推广。针对这一缺口，该项工作围绕STM32H723VGT6微控制器设计并实现了一套低成本的头部佩戴式眼部康复训练装置。

装置选用DM-MC02核心板作为硬件承载平台，整合了BMI088六轴惯性传感器、SG90微型舵机、650nm红色激光模组、以CI1302芯片为核心的语音交互模块以及WS2812可编程状态指示灯。软件层面基于四元数姿态融合框架实现了多策略姿态解算，借助传感器坐标系与头部坐标系的直接映射关系，配合100帧滑动窗口的方差型头稳指标，在10ms的循环周期内完成了佩戴者头部姿态的实时分析。此外开发了一套涵盖九种状态的系统状态机，支持完整训练（五种模式按A至E的顺序自动衔接）与自选训练（通过语音指令逐项选取）两种运行方式。五种临床训练模式——注视稳定性训练、扫视训练、平稳追踪训练、视觉聚焦训练和空间忽略训练——构成了从被动注视到主动视觉搜索的渐进式康复框架。语音交互子系统依托离线识别引擎，提供了10条可识别命令词与46条TTS播报词，结合姿态超限自动暂停、语音紧急中断和跨状态模式切换三道安全防线，保证了训练过程的连续性与操作安全。

测试数据表明，各硬件模块间的通信链路工作正常，姿态估计精度达到了训练任务的要求，五种训练模式均能依照设计逻辑正确运转。该装置为脑卒中后视觉障碍的居家康复训练提供了一套功能完备、操作友好的工程技术路径。

}

\begin{center}
{\zihao{3}\textbf{Design and Implementation of a Head-Mounted \\[2pt]
Ocular Rehabilitation Device for Stroke Patients}}
\end{center}

\vskip 14pt

\begin{center}
{\zihao{3}\textbf{ABSTRACT}}
\end{center}

\vskip 14pt

{\zihao{-4}
Stroke ranks as one of the principal causes of long-term disability across the globe, with roughly 60\% to 73\% of survivors presenting various forms of visual dysfunction~\cite{rowe2021ivis,liu2023vision,zheng2025stroke,sun2024pursuit} — homonymous hemianopsia, oculomotor impairments, and unilateral spatial neglect among them — all of which considerably compromise daily living and rehabilitation prognosis. Existing clinical interventions are caught in a dilemma: conventional manual training lacks standardized procedures and quantitative documentation, while high-end VR/AR-based equipment remains too costly and operationally complex for home-based deployment. To bridge this gap, a low-cost head-mounted ocular rehabilitation device centered on the STM32H723VGT6 microcontroller was designed and implemented in this work.

The device employs a DM-MC02 core board as its hardware foundation, integrating a BMI088 six-axis IMU, SG90 micro servos, a 650\,nm red laser module, a CI1302-based voice interaction unit, and a WS2812 programmable RGB status indicator. At the software level, a quaternion-based attitude fusion framework accomplishes multi-strategy pose estimation; a direct coordinate mapping between the sensor frame and the head frame, combined with a 100-frame sliding-window variance-based head-stability metric, delivers real-time head posture analysis at a 10\,ms cycle rate. A nine-state system state machine was further developed, accommodating two operational modes — Full Mode that chains five training patterns sequentially, and Custom Mode where individual patterns are selected via voice commands. The five clinical training protocols — fixation stability, saccade, smooth pursuit, visual accommodation, and spatial neglect — constitute a progressive rehabilitation framework from passive gaze maintenance to active visual exploration. The voice interaction subsystem, built upon an offline recognition engine, supplies ten recognizable command words and 46 TTS broadcast phrases, and together with a three-tier safety mechanism — posture-exceedance auto-pause, voice-triggered emergency interruption, and cross-state mode switching — ensures both training continuity and operational safety.

Test results confirm that all hardware communication links function normally, the attitude estimation accuracy meets training requirements, and all five training protocols execute correctly according to design logic. The device offers a functionally complete and user-accessible engineering pathway for home-based visual rehabilitation following stroke.

}
